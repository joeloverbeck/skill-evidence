## 2. Recommendation

**Keep your call: `bug` + `ready-for-agent`.** I am applying it, not re-litigating it. But the
transition is unusual in one specific way, and per the skill I have to flag it rather than let an
agent walk into it:

**The fix requires you to ratify a superseding ADR, and only you can do that.** A brief that just
says "drop the line when the count is zero" sends the AFK agent straight into
`brief_report_always_ends_with_a_summary_line`, where its only options are to break the
CONTRIBUTING rule (edit a contract assertion as ordinary bug work) or stall. Two ways out:

- **(a) What I have written the brief to do** — the agent's change must land the superseding ADR
  *in the same change*, drafted as a record of the decision you already made today, and merge is
  gated on your sign-off of that record. You keep the ratification; the agent does the writing and
  the code. This keeps things moving while you are AFK.
- **(b) Stricter split** — `ready-for-human` for you to ratify the ADR, then `ready-for-agent` for
  a purely mechanical change. Cleaner on process, but it stalls until you are back.

I recommend (a), and the brief below is written for it. If you would rather have (b), the brief
converts by deleting the ADR deliverable and re-labelling.

Three smaller points, in descending order of how much they might change your mind:

- **Scope divergence.** #41 asks only about brief. `render_full` has the same behavior and the same
  contract pin. Fixing brief alone means brief and full deliberately disagree at zero items — a
  defensible split (brief is the pipe-friendly surface; full is a document), but it must be a stated
  decision in the ADR, not an accident. The brief scopes to brief and flags the question. If you
  want full to follow, say so and the brief widens by one criterion.
- **Documentation goes stale with the fix.** The glossary defines *summary line* as "the final line
  of a brief report", and `Format::Brief`'s doc comment says "One line per item plus the summary
  line". Both become wrong for the empty case; the brief makes fixing them a criterion.
- **Category, for the record.** Strictly, changing behavior that the repo ratified is closer to
  `enhancement` than `bug` — the code does not diverge from its intent, the intent is being
  overturned. The practical consequence is identical (a superseding ADR either way), and the
  reporter's underlying complaint is sound, so I have kept `bug` as you called it. Worth knowing
  only if it ever gets rejected, since that routes to `.out-of-scope/`.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** A brief report of zero items should render as empty output instead of a lone
`Summary: 0 items` line — and the change must carry a superseding decision record, because the
current behavior is ratified, not accidental.

**Verification:** Confirmed by inspection. The brief renderer appends `Summary: <n> items` plus a
newline after the item lines unconditionally, so an empty item list yields exactly one line of
output; the contract suite asserts that exact string. @dstrand's `wc -l` observation follows
directly. Two notes on the report: the flag as written (`--brief`) is not the CLI's spelling — the
surface is `--format brief`, which is also the default when the argument is absent — and no flag

target, so the suite was read, not run.

**Read this before touching anything:** the empty-brief output is pinned by the contract suite, in
the assertion named `brief_report_always_ends_with_a_summary_line`. Per CONTRIBUTING, contract-suite
assertions encode ratified decisions, are not edited as part of ordinary bug or feature work, and
change only alongside a superseding record in the adopted decision records. Do not delete, rename,
`#[ignore]`, or weaken that assertion to get to a green bar. It may be updated only as part of the
same change that adds the decision record described below, and no other contract assertion may be
touched at all.

**Current behavior:**
The brief renderer emits one line per item and then unconditionally appends the summary line, so an
empty item list renders as `Summary: 0 items` followed by a newline rather than as nothing. The
brief arm of the CLI's report entry point inherits this. Consequences: an empty working set looks
like a result, and line-counting a brief report of nothing returns 1 instead of 0.

**Desired behavior:**
Brief rendering of an empty item list produces the empty string — no summary line, no trailing
newline, no whitespace, zero bytes. With one or more items, brief output is byte-for-byte what it
is today, summary line included. The full and JSON surfaces are unchanged at every item count,
including zero: full still ends with its summary line, and JSON still carries a count of 0.

**Key interfaces:**
- `render_brief(&[Item]) -> String` — returns an empty `String` for an empty slice; identical to
  today for any non-empty slice. No signature change.
- `cli::report(items, Format::Brief)` — inherits the new behavior with no signature change; the
  `Format::Full` and `Format::Json` arms are untouched.
- The doc comment on `Format::Brief` ("One line per item plus the summary line") — restate so it is
  true at zero items. Public functions keep their doc comments.
- The domain glossary entry for **summary line** ("the final line of a brief report") — it is no
  longer unconditional; reword it to say a brief report of at least one item ends with it.
- Contract suite — `brief_report_always_ends_with_a_summary_line` is the single assertion that may
  change (name included, since it will no longer state the truth).
  `brief_report_lists_each_item_before_the_summary_line` and
  `full_report_always_ends_with_a_summary_line` must still pass, unmodified.
- A new record in the adopted decision records, numbered after the highest one present, superseding
  the pinned behavior. It must state: the decision (a brief report of zero items renders nothing);
  the reason (empty output must be genuinely empty, so byte- and line-counting consumers can tell
  "nothing to report" apart from "a result" without parsing); and the consequences (brief and full
  deliberately diverge at zero items; the JSON count field is unaffected and remains the surface for
  machine consumers; any consumer that relied on a brief report always having a trailing line must
  count items instead).

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Brief rendering of zero items returns a zero-length string — not `"\n"`, not a space.
- [ ] Brief rendering of one item and of several items is byte-for-byte unchanged from today,
      still ending with the summary line and its trailing newline.
- [ ] Full rendering of zero items is unchanged, and the JSON surface for zero items still carries
      a count of 0.
- [ ] Going through the CLI report entry point with the brief format gives exactly the same empty

- [ ] Every contract assertion other than `brief_report_always_ends_with_a_summary_line` passes

      name reflects what it now pins.
- [ ] The change includes the new decision record described above, and it supersedes the pinned
      behavior explicitly.
- [ ] The `Format::Brief` doc comment and the glossary's summary-line entry both describe the
      zero-item case correctly.

**Out of scope:**
- Changing the full or JSON surfaces at any item count. The identical summary line on the full

- Adding a `--brief` flag or otherwise changing format parsing, defaults, or exit codes.

- Editing the contract suite beyond the single assertion named above, or adding new contract files.
