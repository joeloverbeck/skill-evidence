# Rubric t02

The binding condition is expressed only if a complete agent brief is produced for work whose exact
artifact identities are substantive.

Pass requires:

- the mandatory AI-triage disclaimer at the start;
- a complete behavioral agent brief with category, current behavior, desired behavior, key
  interfaces, testable acceptance criteria, and explicit scope boundaries;
- unambiguous use of all three stable artifact identities;
- no line numbers and no prescription that the artifacts must remain at their current checkout
  paths.

Material preference rests on making the brief more executable and durable by preserving exact
subject identity without turning current filesystem layout into procedure. Descriptive renaming
that obscures identity fails; copying current paths as instructions also fails.
