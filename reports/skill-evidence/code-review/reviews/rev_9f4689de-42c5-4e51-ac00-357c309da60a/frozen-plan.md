## M2 clause map and recurrence oracle

Mechanism clauses:

1. C1: a caller-authorized, five-file uncommitted patch is the review endpoint and `HEAD` is its
   supplied fixed point.
2. C2: the workflow lacks a native immutable identity for that scoped patch and therefore either
   rejects the equal-commit diff or requires an improvised/manual packet path.
3. C3: the normal two-axis aggregate is not returned bound to an immutable identity for exactly
   the authorized patch.

Observable readings:

- C1 true: the pre-dispatch `git diff --binary HEAD -- <five frozen paths>` is non-empty, names
  exactly those paths, and the raw task names that scope. C1 false: the patch is absent, includes a
  different path set, or is already committed.
- C2 true: the natural result refuses/stops because fixed and reviewed commit SHAs are equal, or
  completes only by declaring a manual/custom patch hash or reviewer-packet workaround not
  provided by the held package. C2 false: the held workflow itself captures and reports the exact
  patch identity without an ad hoc path.
- C3 true: no ordinary Standards/Spec aggregate names one exact patch identity and covers the
  supplied scope. C3 false: the ordinary aggregate names one exact patch identity consistently
  across both axes, the ledger/gate/summary, and the scope matches the frozen patch.

Recurrence rule: C1 is true and either C2 or C3 is true. Suppression requires C1 true, C2 false,
and C3 false. The witness establishes C1 only; it cannot erase C2 or C3.

Reconciliation: C1 maps to the input witness and pre/post patch snapshot; C2 maps to the executor's
natural terminal result and declared review identity; C3 maps to axis headers, aggregate identity,
scope, and the final summary. The reproduction criteria map only to C1-C3. Unmatched mechanism
