### What this result does not claim

3/5 versus 0/5 is a consistent direction, not a significant result on its own (Fisher exact
p ≈ 0.17). Two things support reading it as real rather than noise: the mechanism of action above,
and the fact that both blind evaluators, scoring a pooled shuffled set with the arm map withheld,
agreed on all ten verdicts and on the violating text in every case.

Both evaluators also independently flagged one caveat, recorded here rather than buried: candidate
run 2 carries a "regression guard outside the loop" subsection whose red position holds prose. Both
declined to count it as an evidence row — it is declared not a cycle, is unnumbered, and its own
reconciliation names its evidence row as `none` — and one stated that counting it would flip that
document to `VIOLATION`. The worst-case reading is therefore **candidate 1/5 against current 3/5**,
still an improvement and a less decisive one.
