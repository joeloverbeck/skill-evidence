## Mechanism of action

All five candidate-arm runs adopted a defined command-key scheme with the argument supplied, against
two of five on the current arm. The candidate did not merely forbid the abbreviation; it moved runs
to the structure that makes abbreviation unnecessary.

## Recorded caveat

Both evaluators independently flagged candidate run 2: a "regression guard outside the loop"
subsection whose red position holds prose. Both declined to score it as an evidence row — it is
declared not a red→green cycle, carries no row number, and its own reconciliation entry names its
TDD evidence row as `none` — and one stated that counting it would flip that document to
`VIOLATION`. The worst-case reading is therefore **candidate 1/5 vs current 3/5**, still an
improvement but a less decisive one. The headline reading is 0/5 vs 3/5, and both are recorded here
rather than only the favourable one.

Current-arm run 2 carried the same green-on-arrival disclosure *inside* its numbered evidence table
(row 5b), which is why it was scored as a row there. That is a difference in where each run placed
its disclosure, not a difference the rubric was designed to detect.

## Statistical honesty

3/5 versus 0/5 is a consistent direction, not a significant result on its own (Fisher exact
p ≈ 0.17). The acceptance gate does not ask for significance; it asks whether the candidate resolves
the mechanism on the reproduction cases while staying noninferior elsewhere. The supporting reason
to believe the split is real rather than noise is the mechanism of action above, which is visible in
every candidate run.
