## Reading this close rests on

Step 5 offers two readings of a passing arm and forbids recording both. This close rests on the
**first**: the witness was expressed on every run and the failure still did not appear, so the
target handles the condition the arm reproduced. It does **not** rest on
`blocked_no_valid_test` — no run went unexpressed, so the arm is informative about the incidents
rather than silent on them.

What the arm reproduced, concretely: a fresh executor holding this skill, given a multi-behavior
task whose natural implementation invites a guard no current assertion requires (T1) or whose
obvious single implementation satisfies all six listed behaviors at once (T2). What it did: wrote
one focused failing test per behavior, observed the intended failure, then implemented minimally —
8 times out of 8, with zero first-run-green tests.

What this close deliberately does **not** claim: that the incidents were not real, or that no
mechanism exists. Both triggers are recorded material failures. The distinguishing condition between
them and these trials is what the trials could not carry — evt_5930fbff's defect went undetected
across eight further cycles and six review passes on a 14-file change, and evt_ff458eea's across a
production-implementation phase and an R1 repair. A fresh short-context executor reproduced the
*decision point* faithfully and passed it; it cannot reproduce a *detection distance* measured in
review passes. That is a genuine limit on what this arm establishes, recorded here rather than
converted into a verdict either way. If the mechanism is real, it should recur under real conditions
and reopen the gate on its own evidence — which is what `monitor_for_recurrence` is for.
