# Domain Ruling

## Disposition

Reject the proposal.

`inputs/repository/CONTEXT.md` expressly says that authority language is governed by `docs/principles/README.md` and that the lifecycle glossary does not restate it. Adding the proposed definition would duplicate an upstream concept in the downstream glossary and defeat that deference rule.

## Owning source

The owning source for **Authorization** and authoritative-state language is `inputs/repository/docs/principles/README.md`, the repository constitution. Its rule is that human intent, a constrained proposal, applicable checks, inspectable consequences, and explicit human acceptance must precede authoritative state.

## Domain-document outcome

No domain-document update is needed. `CONTEXT.md` already records the correct ownership boundary, and the proposal does not establish a new lifecycle-layer term. No ADR is warranted because no architectural or structural choice remains to be recorded.

Provenance: adjudicated `inputs/proposal.md` against `inputs/repository/CONTEXT.md` and `inputs/repository/docs/principles/README.md`.
