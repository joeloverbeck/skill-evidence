# Domain Ruling

## Disposition

**Reject the proposal.** `CONTEXT.md` explicitly says that authority language is governed by `docs/principles/README.md` and that the glossary does not restate it. Adding the proposed definition would violate that deference rule, and the proposal's aim of avoiding consultation of the constitution would bypass the governing source.

The proposed wording is also not an adequate substitute for the constitution. “Permission to make a state authoritative” collapses a rule that requires human intent, a constrained proposal, applicable checks, inspectable consequences, and explicit human acceptance before authoritative state.

## Owning source

`inputs/repository/docs/principles/README.md` owns authority language, including any repository-level meaning assigned to “Authorization.” The term is not a lifecycle-layer term owned by `CONTEXT.md`.

## Domain-document outcome

No domain-document update is needed. Do not add the proposed entry to `CONTEXT.md`: its existing preamble already places the term with the constitution. No ADR is warranted because no new architectural or structural decision has crystallized; this ruling applies an existing ownership boundary.

Convention checked: the glossary's explicit deference rule for authority language. No mirror, formatter, validator, or pointer update applies to this ruling artifact.
