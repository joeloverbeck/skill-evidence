  never state *what a selection ratifies* when the chosen option carried a preview body. On a
  fresh arm this reproduced with a sharp signature: every run re-opened the preview-body item

  Premise verification, not ratification scope, is doing all the work; where the environment is
  silent, the preview body ratifies itself. This is `evt_8d22ad3a`'s mechanism.

  reaching the step (`evt_87382a03`'s shape). On this instrument, that is evidence the wording is
  followed when freshly read, and the candidate was therefore scoped to the shape that
